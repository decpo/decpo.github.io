Active Sentence Processing

A-maze versus Self-Paced Reading Paradigms: Capturing Individual Differences in Sentence Processing

Two widely used paradigms in sentence processing, A-maze and self-paced reading, have recently been used to investigate individual differences in sentence processing. This project aims to compare the reliability of each task across two testing sessions.
